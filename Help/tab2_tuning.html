Tuning

Select a row to apply that point as the current tone (freq/ampl/phase). The selected tone then becomes the device output.

Controls
  • Stop tone clears the calibration tone.
  • Amplifier toggle enables/disables RF output (default OFF on startup).
  • Sync channel wipers ties CH1 & CH2 wiper sliders together.
  • DDS Power controls the power of the DDS output.
  • Channel Wipers
    • Channel 1 controls the wiper voltage of Channel 1.
    • Channel 2 controls the wiper voltage of Channel 2.
  • LUT Interpolation determines how the points in the LUT are connected.
    • Spot Sets each point to its exact value. else zero
    • Step holds the specified value until the next point.
    • Linear connects the points with straight lines.
    • LinearExtend extends the first and last points to the boundaries.
    • Cubic uses cubic interpolation for smoother transitions.
  • Selected Point Edit (seen in the output in realtime)
    • Amplitude Edits the amplitude of the selected point.
    • Phase Edits the phase offset at the selected point.
Beam-steer best practice

When tuning a beam steered deflector we recommend the following best practices:

  • Adjust for peak efficiency: PHASE first and then AMPLITUDE.
  • Aim for maximum deflected optical power without overdrive.

Slider wheel steps: normal = 0.1, Ctrl = fine, Shift = coarse (5× page step).
